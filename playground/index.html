Playground

Things to try. Each one opens the working experiment.

  • Soft Matter ↗Pull and squish a slime.Iris
  • Resonance ↗Pluck a fabric that sings.Iris
  • Afterimage ↗Expose and dodge a print.Iris
  • TONUS ↗Play a synthesizer from your keyboard.Synth
  • Orbit ↗Move sounds around your head.Spatial audio
  • Hinge ↗Drag a card and tune its spring.Motion

The three Iris pieces build on Holocloth by Dmitry Kurash (MIT). What is reused and what is new is set out in the Iris case study.
