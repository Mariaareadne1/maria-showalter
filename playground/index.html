Playground

Things to pull, pluck and expose.

Experiments, not products. Each one opens the working piece; most want a mouse or a finger, and two make sound only after you touch them.

  1. PullSoft Matter ↗A slime you can pull, poke and squish — a skin that accepts a stretch, then forgets it.Iris · live · opens the live experiment
  2. PluckResonance ↗A fabric you play as an instrument; the ripples are a damped wave equation and the sound comes from the same physics.Iris · live · opens the live experiment
  3. ExposeAfterimage ↗A darkroom where prints develop; dodging and burning edit a map of exposure time.Iris · live · opens the live experiment
  4. playPlayTONUS ↗A polyphonic synthesizer you play from the keyboard; four synthesis methods crossfaded live.Live · opens the live experiment
  5. mixMixDJ deckTwo decks, filters and an equal-power crossfader, on this site.On the homepage · opens the deck on this site

The three Iris pieces build on Holocloth by Dmitry Kurash (MIT). What is reused and what is new is set out in the Iris case study.
