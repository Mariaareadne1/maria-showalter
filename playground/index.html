Playground

Small worlds and material studies. Every one is live; most take a single gesture.

Painted worlds

Draw something and it comes alive. Each opens the live piece.

  • MeadowDraw a loop on the grass and it becomes a sheep.
  • Pond ↗Loops on the water turn into koi, ducks and frogs.Source for Pond
  • Hive ↗Draw a flower in the garden; the bees come to visit.Source for Hive
  • Lanterns ↗Draw a lantern over the lake at dusk and let it drift up.Source for Lanterns
  • Tide ↗Draw in the wet sand; little crabs walk your line, and the sea takes it back.Source for Tide
  • Bubbles ↗Draw a circle and it becomes a soap bubble with something asleep inside. Tap to pop.Source for Bubbles
  • Sky ↗Draw a line over a painted town and a paper plane flies it.Source for Sky

Studies

Light, wind and cloth, one material at a time.

  • Wind Study ↗Draw a line across a painted meadow and a gust travels through 360,000 grass strands.Source for Wind Study
  • Somewhere Else ↗Take hold of a heavy printed tapestry and lift it off the world to find another one under it.
  • Light Study 001 ↗Move across a storefront and the hour moves with you, from morning to night.
  • Light Study 002 ↗Move the sun across a folded slab of stone and through its horizon opening.

Iris

Studies in digital tactility.

  • Iris · Resonance ↗Pluck or pull a holographic fabric and it sounds.Source for Iris · Resonance
  • Iris · Soft Matter ↗Pull and dent a blob of holographic slime.Source for Iris · Soft Matter

The Iris pieces grow out of Iris, an adaptation of Holocloth by Dmitry Kurash (MIT). Resonance and Afterimage keep its Verlet cloth structure and grain pass; Soft Matter keeps the grain pass and an adapted foil shader. Every repository's licence keeps his copyright line. Holocloth ↗
