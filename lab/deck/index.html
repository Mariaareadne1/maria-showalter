Deck

A two-deck mixer that runs in the page. Load the two loops, or your own audio files. Nothing plays until you press Play.

Tempo
Changes playback rate; pitch follows, as on a turntable.
Sync
Sets both decks to the tempo shown.
Platter
Drag to nudge a playing deck faster or slower.
Filters
Low pass and high pass on both decks.
Crossfader
Equal-power blend from A to B.

Files you open stay in your browser. The two loops are original, synthesised for this site.
