Design engineer · creative technologist

I build interactive software that behaves like material — springs you can tune, cloth you can play, prints that develop — and the tools that turn it into something people can use.

01

Selected work

Six projects, chosen for what can be tried today. Software first; experiments are marked as experiments.

FrameFlow

Turns a raw macOS screen recording into a composed product film. Clicks and pauses become editable camera Moments; one renderer drives preview, MP4 and GIF, so the export is exactly what you saw.

macOS app · Electron · React · Swift (ScreenCaptureKit) · WebCodecs
Sole designer and engineer · v2.3.0, not notarized
Case study →GitHub ↗
  1. 02

    Hinge

    Tune hover, press and drag by handling a component and shaping its spring, then export a React component that moves exactly as it did on the stage.

    Web app · React · TypeScript · spring solver
    Live demo ↗GitHub ↗
    Capture predates Hinge's current redesign
  2. 03

    Iris

    Three browser studies in digital tactility: a slime you pull, a fabric you play as an instrument, a darkroom where prints develop.

    Series · three.js · Web Audio · custom simulation · after Holocloth by Dmitry Kurash
    Case study →Try the three →
  3. 04

    Edition

    A photograph becomes a two-ink print: halftone plates at their own angles, overprinted and deliberately out of register, exported at full resolution.

    Web app · TypeScript · Web Workers · image processing
    Live demo ↗GitHub ↗
    Capture predates Edition's current redesign
  4. 05

    Orbit

    Choreograph sounds around a listener. Positions drive real HRTF panning; the arrangement renders to a WAV that pans the way playback did.

    Web app · Web Audio · TypeScript · headphones
    Live demo ↗GitHub ↗
    Capture predates Orbit's current redesign
  5. 06

    Filo

    A campus map of places ranked by people you trust: rank by comparison in about log₂(n) taps, with privacy enforced in the database.

    Mobile prototype · React Native · Supabase · not released
    Case study →GitHub ↗
