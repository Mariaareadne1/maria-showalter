I

Instruments

Tools you learn by handling. Each exports something that behaves the way the instrument did.

01

Hinge

Tune hover, press and drag by handling a component and shaping its spring, then export a React component that moves exactly as it did on the stage.

Web app · React · TypeScript · closed-form spring solver
Live demo ↗GitHub ↗
02

Orbit

Choreograph sounds around a listener. Positions drive real HRTF panning; the arrangement renders to a WAV that pans the way playback did.

Web Audio · TypeScript · use headphones
Live demo ↗GitHub ↗

Capture predates Orbit's current redesign.

II

Materials

What makes something on a screen feel physical — or on paper, printed.

03

Iris

Three studies in digital tactility: a slime you pull, a fabric you play as an instrument, a darkroom where prints develop. Built on Holocloth by Dmitry Kurash (MIT).

Case study →Try them →
04

Edition

A photograph becomes a two-ink print: halftone plates at their own angles, exported at full resolution from the same engine as the preview.

TypeScript · Web Workers · runs in the browser
Live demo ↗GitHub ↗
III

Products

Software with users in mind, documented with what is and isn't finished.

05

FrameFlow

Turns a raw macOS screen recording into a composed product film. One renderer drives preview, MP4 and GIF.

Electron · React · Swift (ScreenCaptureKit) · WebCodecs · v2.3.0, not notarized
Case study →GitHub ↗
06

Filo

A campus map of places ranked by people you trust, with privacy enforced in the database. Prototype; not released.

React Native · Supabase · Mapbox
