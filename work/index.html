Work

Instruments, products and studies.

I design and build the whole thing — interaction, rendering, audio, the data model — and check that what you feel on screen is what ships.

Most projects start from a feeling I want a control to have — weight, give, a print's grain — and end in an exporter or a test that proves the result matches the preview. I write the physics and DSP myself when the feel depends on it, and document what is unfinished.
