04

Two decks

A small DJ mixer that runs in the page. Every control is wired to the audio graph; nothing plays until you press play.

I DJ on a Pioneer DDJ-400 and perform live-coded sets at Algoraves. This is the browser version: pick a track per deck, set the tempo, filter and blend.

Tempo
Changes playback rate; pitch follows, as on a turntable.
Sync
Sets both decks to the tempo shown.
Platter
Drag to nudge a playing deck faster or slower.
Filters
Low pass and high pass on both decks.
Crossfader
Equal-power blend from A to B.
